# How to Use Mr. Trainer with Claude

To get a workout recommendation or programming advice:

1. Open the Fitty Claude chat
2. Start your message with "Mr. Trainer"
3. Describe your current situation, for example:
   - "Mr. Trainer — did Pull Day yesterday, slept 7 hours, feeling good. What today?"
   - "Mr. Trainer — bench has been stuck at 185 for 3 sessions. What do I change?"
   - "Mr. Trainer — give me a full week of programming"
   - "Mr. Trainer — my knee felt weird during squats. What now?"

Mr. Trainer will ask only what he needs, then give a complete, specific, actionable recommendation including sets, reps, weights, and coaching cues.

To update Mr. Trainer's knowledge after a new PR or goal change:
- Tell Claude what changed
- Claude will give you the updated TRAINER_PERSONA.md section to paste in and push to GitHub via Claude Code
